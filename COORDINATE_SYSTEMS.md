# Coordinate systems

Every automatic estimate uses image, intrinsics, depth/confidence, timestamp, and camera transform copied from the same `ARFrame`; smoothed scene depth is preferred when ARKit provides it. RealityKit also attempts intersections against the current reconstructed world mesh, but mesh availability is optional. Every mesh ray is built from the snapshot's intrinsics and camera transform, and stale detections are rejected before fusion.

| Space | Origin and axes | Units | Conversion |
|---|---|---|---|
| EXIF-oriented Vision image | Bottom-left; +x right, +y up | 0...1 | Detection runs with portrait `.right` orientation. `ImageCoordinateMapper` returns every physical plate corner to raw coordinates. |
| Raw captured image | Bottom-left normalized; pixels use top-left | 0...1 / pixels | Raw normalized points feed depth/pose. Pixels use `x = raw.x × width`, `y = (1 − raw.y) × height`. |
| Scene-depth pixels | Top-left, matching the captured-image aspect | pixels | Captured pixels scale by depth/image dimensions; interior points avoid boundary contamination. |
| AR camera | Optical center; +x right, +y up, camera looks along −z | metres | `[(u−cx)z/fx, −(v−cy)z/fy, −z, 1]`. Intrinsics use ARKit's column-major matrix (`fx = K[0,0]`, `fy = K[1,1]`, `cx = K[2,0]`, `cy = K[2,1]`). |
| AR world | Session origin; gravity-aligned +y up | metres | Multiply homogeneous camera point by `ARCamera.transform`. |
| Clip/NDC | Metal clip z is 0...w | normalized | `viewProjection × world`, divide by w; w ≤ 0 is behind the camera. |
| SwiftUI screen | Top-left; +x right, +y down | points | NDC x/y maps into viewport; card layout may displace the card but retains a leader line to the projected point. |

`simd_float4x4` values are column-major. Translation is column 3. A vehicle card attaches 0.75 m above its automatically measured base point along gravity-aligned world +y. This presentation offset does not change the base `ARAnchor`.

For crop OCR, the raw quadrilateral is mapped back to portrait coordinates and applied to an EXIF-oriented `CIImage`; this keeps semantic plate corners and Core Image's geometric corners aligned. The full-frame text-localization path receives Vision observations in that same EXIF-oriented normalized space, expands a validated registration's text box to include the plate field, clips it to the image, and then follows the normal appearance/raw-coordinate path. No detector geometry is rendered into the consumer interface.

Depth values must be finite and 0.4–12 m. Automatic sampling admits low-confidence reflective-plate cells only when at least five coherent samples survive; otherwise at least three medium/high-confidence cells are required. Sampling deduplicates physical depth cells, and a median absolute-deviation pass removes edge contamination. The projected plate center is the diagonals' projective intersection, not the four-corner arithmetic mean. The four-corner solution must stay within `max(0.35 m, 18%)` of depth, below 30% axis-scale mismatch, below 0.18 orthogonality error, and below 5 px rigid reprojection error. When mesh samples exist, at least three distinct rays must form a spatially broad, planar surface with a compatible normal, within 28 cm of the depth center and 40 cm of the planar center. Missing or degenerate mesh is optional; a broad reconstructed surface more than `max(0.45 m, 12%)` from the accepted depth pose vetoes placement. Pose fusion needs at least three inliers within 18 cm and 15°, while automatic commitment requires five observations, four pose samples spanning 0.25 seconds, and two recent `.normal`-tracking poses. The fused pose—not the first raw pose—becomes the stationarity reference. The resulting transform creates the world anchor before OCR. Detector expiry can remove screen-space tracking metadata, but it does not alter or delete that anchor. If ARKit later refines the anchor during map adjustment, that authoritative transform is synchronized into reassociation while identity remains unchanged.
