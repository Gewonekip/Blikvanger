# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender
identity or expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include
respectful disagreement, accepting responsibility, and focusing on what is
best for the project. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, intimidation,
public or private personal attacks, publishing private information, and other
conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers. Reports
will be reviewed and handled fairly and confidentially where possible.
