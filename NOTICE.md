# Notices

Blikvanger uses Apple system frameworks including SwiftUI, ARKit, RealityKit,
Vision, Core Image, and AVFoundation. Those frameworks remain subject to
Apple's terms and are not relicensed by this repository.

Vehicle information is read from the [Open Data RDW dataset](https://opendata.rdw.nl/Voertuigen/Open-Data-RDW-Gekentekende_voertuigen/m9d7-ebf2).
Review the dataset's current terms and RDW privacy statement before deploying
a fork or changing the data use.
